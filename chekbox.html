CSS Grid: auto vs fr

1. The "auto" Logic (Content First)

auto column sirf utni jagah leta hai jitni uske andar ke content ko chahiye. Agar content chota hai, toh column chota ho jayega.

Yahan har box apne text ke hisaab se adjust ho raha hai. Baki space khali bach sakti hai.

2. The "fr" Logic (Available Space First)

1fr ka matlab hai "bachi hui poori space ko barabar baant do". Ye content ko nahi dekhta, ye container ki width ko dekhta hai.

1fr
Yahan teeno boxes barabar hain, bhale hi text chota ho ya bara.

3. Professional Scenario (Fixed + Auto + Flexible)

Asli maza tab aata hai jab hum inhein mix karte hain. common pattern: 200px auto 1fr

Sidebar (200px)
Middle (Auto)
Main Content (1fr - Takes all remaining space)
  • 200px: Hamesha fix rahega.
  • auto: Jitna middle text hoga, utni jagah lega.
  • 1fr: Jitni bhi baki screen bachi, ye us par qabza kar lega.
